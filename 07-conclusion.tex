\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{ch:conclusion}

% Drafted 2026-09-30 by an external LLM from prompts/PROMPT_final_chapters.md
% and audited line by line against that fact sheet (see NOTES.md).

This thesis set out to answer one question: \emph{What is needed to
forecast the progression of Geographic Atrophy from longitudinal OCT?} It
was split into two parts: (1) What kind of framework does the forecast
need? (2) What kind of operator does that framework need?

\paragraph{The framework.}
The forecast needs a setup that absorbs the irregularity of clinical data
and treats every candidate model the same way. The framework built in
Chapter~\ref{ch:method} does this with a residual update conditioned on
the elapsed time $\Delta t$, a zero-initialised output so that every model
starts from persistence, a training curriculum measured in elapsed days,
and one loss and one evaluation for all models. Held fixed, it served
operators from unrelated literatures without change, and every operator
with spatial context learned to predict change far above persistence.
How time enters the update mattered less than that it enters: a
higher-order Runge--Kutta step bought nothing over a single Euler step.

\paragraph{The operator.}
The architecture class does not decide the outcome. The three strongest
operators come from three unrelated classes --- a finite-element network,
a graph network and a U-Net --- and lie close together, at a
change-region Dice of about 0.50 to 0.54 at one year, against zero for
persistence. What separates operators are a few properties that were
measured one at a time.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Spatial context.} Without it, a model predicts no change at
    all; one ring of neighbours raises the change-region Dice from 0 to
    about 0.45.
  \item \textbf{Physical reach at the scale of the lesion front.}
    Changing only where the neighbours of a graph network lie, so that one
    step reaches as far as the front moves between visits, raised the
    change-region Dice by 0.064 at an unchanged parameter count. It is the
    largest effect in the project.
  \item \textbf{An explicit transport term.} Adding a learned transport
    term to a Finite Element Network raised it by 0.062 over the same
    network without it, and by 0.072 over a parameter-matched control on
    the four folds on which that control trained.
  \item \textbf{Global and local context together.} A purely global
    operator lies below the U-Net at one year; a local path of 800
    parameters brings it level.
\end{itemize}
Reach and transport are two unrelated ways to correct the same deficit:
a single update that cannot see as far as the disease moves between two
visits. That two independent routes lead to the same level is the central
architectural result of this thesis, more informative than the score of
any single model.

\paragraph{What did not matter.}
The negative results are part of the answer. Larger models did not help in
any of the four classes tested, and the widest U-Net became worse. A
higher-order time integration did not help. The moving-mesh extension,
reported in Appendix~\ref{app:mm-pde}, did not improve accuracy, because
its correction branch never engaged. The validity diagnostics also limit
what may be said: the canonical model is a one-step map conditioned on
the length of the step, not a continuous-time model. Models that do
behave as continuous-time models can be trained, but they are not more
accurate.

\paragraph{Clinical position.}
The operators predict where the lesion changes better than how fast an
individual eye grows. On growth speed, the canonical model reaches a
correlation of 0.40 with the true growth rates, against 0.61 for the
model of \citet{Mai2024} on the same cohort, which starts from the raw OCT
volume where this thesis starts from segmented masks. The two results are
not directly comparable, but the gap shows where the approach is weakest.
The small cohort, the censoring of the fixed crop and the missing memory
of past visits bound every conclusion drawn here.

\paragraph{Take-away.}
For forecasts from longitudinal medical imaging in a low-data clinical
setting, what matters is the framework around the model and the
properties of the operator placed in it --- above all, whether one update
can see as far as the disease moves between two visits --- rather than
the choice of operator family.
